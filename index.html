Quarterback supply

In a 12-team superflex, 24 starting quarterback slots exist and roughly 20 quarterbacks are startable. That gap is the whole game. Every quarterback that leaves the board makes yours worth more and the next manager more desperate.

How to play the room

  • Leave with two you trust. Two real quarterbacks by round six beats a prettier bench every time.
  • Watch for runs. Two quarterbacks off the board back to back means a tier is emptying. Jump in rather than wait for the next one.
  • Don't chase the third. Once you have two starters, a third quarterback is a luxury unless it costs almost nothing or the value is absurd.
  • Josh Allen is the one safe anchor. He is alone in the top tier this year. Everyone else at the position carries a real question.
  • The second quarterback tier is six deep. Maye, Jackson, Daniels, Hurts, Burrow, Williams. If you miss all six, Herbert, Prescott, Goff and Stafford still start for you.
  • Handcuffs wait. Two quarterback slots eat bench space. Backup running backs are a last-three-rounds problem.
